# Petsy

**Petsy** is a one-stop dress shop for cats and dogs, designed to provide pet owners with a convenient and enjoyable shopping experience. Our platform offers a wide selection of stylish and comfortable clothing for your furry friends.

### Key Features

- **User Authentication**: Secure registration and login/logout functionality.
- **Product Catalog**: Browse a diverse range of pet apparel for cats and dogs.
- **MERN Stack**: Built using MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, and Node.js for a robust full-stack application.

Petsy aims to be the ultimate destination for pet fashion, ensuring that your beloved pets look their best!
